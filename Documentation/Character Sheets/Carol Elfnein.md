## Identity and core statistics

Human (2024), Sage; Evoker Wizard 12 / Alchemist Artificer 3, total level 15. Wizard was the starting class. Artificial-body appearance is lore; no Reborn mechanics or automatic extra life. Human supplies Alert; Sage supplies Magic Initiate (Wizard). Wizard feat choices are +2 INT, War Caster (+1 INT) and Skill Expert (+1 DEX).

## Spell resources and books

Shared caster level is 14: Wizard 12 +ceil(Artificer 3/2). Slots by level 1–7 are **4/3/3/3/2/1/1**. A Long Rest restores expended slots. Actual access is Wizard through level 6 and Artificer through level 1; the seventh-level slot supports upcasts, not seventh-level spell acquisition.

Arcane Recovery restores up to six combined slot levels after a Short Rest, once per Long Rest, excluding level 6+ slots.

Campaign books hold 16 entries each, including cantrips; eligible contents are ready. More books add capacity, not slots or class cantrip choices. All three are permanent Enduring Spellbooks, protecting the books and writing from fire, water and aging, with no attunement or additional capacity. Total selected entries: **22 leveled spells +10 cantrips = 32**.

Magic Initiate gives the existing Mage Armor entry one free level 1 cast per Long Rest; extra casts use slots. Normal duration and components remain. This adds a casting source, not another entry or slot pool.

## Features in use

Evoker Potent Cantrip supplies half damage on the applicable failed damaging-cantrip attack/successful target save, without extra effects. Sculpt Spells protects selected visible creatures against applicable Evocation saves/damage. Empowered Evocation adds INT +6 to one damage roll per qualifying Wizard Evocation casting, without a daily pool. Overchannel needs Wizard 14 and is unavailable.

Evocation Savant's acquisition benefits and Memorize Spell's preparation swap are partly superseded or redundant under the campaign book policy. Scholar, book rituals and Arcane Recovery remain. There is no Bladesong, Bladesinger Extra Attack, Sorcery Points, Metamagic, Alchemical Savant or Flash of Genius.

Artificer grants Mending and temporary mundane creation through Tinker's Magic, six uses per Long Rest at INT +6. Respect tool-in-hand casting requirements; a component pouch alone does not replace them. Known plans are Wand +1, Manifold, Goggles of Night and Alchemy Jug. Usual replicas are Goggles/Jug; permanent Wand/Manifold do not consume those two allowances. Alchemist halves potion crafting time. See [Items and Elixirs](../Class%20Reference/Carol%20Artificer%20Items%20and%20Elixirs.md) for elixir rules and provenance.

## Faust / Otherworldly Guise

Faust is the appearance of the retained Tasha spell, not an item or Bladesinger feature. Casting uses a Bonus Action, concentration up to one minute (ten rounds), and a reusable 500 gp engraved planar object, which is owned; cosmetic form is open.

The spell grants 40 ft flight, +2 AC, magical weapon attacks using casting ability, and two attacks in the Attack action. Choose Lower Planes for fire/poison damage immunity and Poisoned immunity, or Upper Planes for radiant/necrotic damage immunity and Charmed immunity. Spellcasting remains possible. Mage Armor plus Guise is AC 18, or 23 while Shield applies. The source is a community Tasha transcription, not a claimed revised replacement.

## Aetherlock summary

Custom Artificer-attuned Two-Handed rifle, no Light property, range 80/240 ft, six unrestricted chambers. Proficient INT aim is +11; Basic damage is 2d10 +6 Piercing. Each permitted attack fires 1–3 Basic/Arcane projectiles or one Spellround/HE-FRAG. Guise provides two attack opportunities; fresh True Strike instead uses its normal Magic action for one enhanced projectile. There is no free True Strike added to a Guise burst.

Physical loading and chamber/fuze selection have no separate action cost. Replacing a charge canister costs a Bonus Action and immediately locks the rifle through the entire next own turn; it is ready at the start of the second subsequent own turn. This also blocks rifle Shield reactions. Rifle weight and initial loaded order remain pending.

One actual scroll mounted in a 1 gp Basic Round supplies a Spellround. Scribing creates a new item; insertion/removal only handles an existing item. Scribing and installation/removal each multiply their own baseline cost and time by M = 3^((5 − total Arcana)/5). Level 1+ handling uses 50 gp/two hours per level before M; cantrip handling uses 15 gp/one eight-hour workday before M. Specific consumed components stay full price and are paid once during scribing, not again on release. At Carol's +16, a new Cone of Cold round is about 157.09 gp and a Shield/Cure Wounds round about 7.69 gp, before firing power. See the [cost ledger](../Class%20Reference/Aetherlock%20Costs%20and%20Damage.md).

Scrolls save donor formulas, DC, scaling and applicable bonuses, rolled on release. Current book casting instead uses the current caster's requirements, resources and statistics without changing the stored snapshot. Do not combine bonus sets. Scroll mounting/removal preserves identity. Anyone can use a one-shot scroll under the campaign exception. Firing pays no new payload slot; rests do not refill ammunition. Carol maintains required concentration after release.

Impact releases at first contact; Airburst uses a selected point unless earlier contact intervenes; Self affects the holder without a projectile. Compatible cones originate at release, with direction chosen at firing. Valid-target rules, normal attacks/saves and friendly fire remain. No additional AC gate applies to save-only areas; misses have DM-determined collision. Direct casts retain normal Sculpt Spells, stored rounds do not. Multi-ray spells retain separate attacks.

Rifle Shield uses a Reaction, an already-held/ready rifle and a loaded round. Self needs no aim; willing ally delivery uses aim against DC 10 under normal range/attack rules. Success grants normal Shield protection, including the triggering hit, until the start of Carol's next turn. Failure consumes the round, charges and Reaction. There is no general offensive reaction fire or free reload.

HE-FRAG deals 5d6 Piercing in a 20 ft radius, DC 15 DEX half, without Basic damage or Evoker bonus. The selected recipe uses one 100 gp grenade, six Basic Rounds and 20 gp parts; one hour of Tinker work produces six rounds at 21 gp each.
