## Personal Self payloads — selected fuze modes

Selected 2026-10-17: the same compatible round can be programmed when firing to **Self** or **Impact**. Self releases the stored effect onto the rifle's wielder without launching a projectile; it does not require pointing the rifle at oneself. Impact launches the round and applies the effect to the hit target. This explicitly permits transferring a personal Self spell to another target as an Aetherlock house rule. No separate blank recipe is required. Existing area-cone impact/airburst rules remain.

Changing delivery does not change the stored spell, donor snapshot, paid slot or manufacture cost. Instantaneous/one-round eligibility still applies; this decision does not admit longer-duration buffs. Ordinary spell attack/save resolution remains selected; exceptions for originally Self spells, unwilling recipients and reaction triggers need explicit review rather than inventing a universal hit/save rule. Shield's selected reaction-release exception is below. Other reaction payloads are not automatically approved; no free additional reaction is granted.
## Shield reaction release — selected timing and aiming requirement

Selected 2026-10-17: Carol can spend her Reaction to discharge a loaded Shield round when she or a visible ally is hit. User explicitly REQUIRES an aiming roll, superseding the no-aim recommendation above for this recipe. On successful delivery, Shield applies before triggering-hit damage and lasts until the start of Carol's next turn. Consume the round and Reaction on discharge; failure does not deliver protection. Rifle must already be held/ready; no reload or additional Reaction granted. Self/Impact fuze delivery remains selected. This is a narrow custom Shield rule, not permission to fire arbitrary offensive payloads as reactions. SELECTED ally delivery threshold: fixed DC10 for a willing recipient, using Carol's gun aim d20+11, rather than recipient AC. No ordinary bullet damage. This remains a gun aiming attack, with normal attack-roll natural-1 and range rules; do not implement it as a guaranteed ability check. Self mode SELECTED: no aiming roll, because no projectile is launched; Reaction and loaded round still consumed. Normal support-spell automatic targeting remains the default outside this explicitly modified Shield recipe.
