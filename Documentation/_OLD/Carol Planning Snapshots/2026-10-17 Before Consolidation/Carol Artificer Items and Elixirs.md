## Elixirs at Artificer3

Two random elixirs after Long Rest while holding Alchemist's Supplies. D6=6 chooses an effect. Bonus Action to drink/administer within5ft. A Magic action and a spell slot make one chosen extra elixir; higher slots do not automatically make more or stronger elixirs. Unused elixirs vanish at next Long Rest; no permanent stockpile. No concentration requirement stated.

| Effect | Carol at Artificer3 |

| Swiftness |Speed+10ft,1hour|
| Resilience |AC+1,10minutes|
| Boldness |+1d4 to attack rolls/saves,1minute|
| Flight |Fly10ft,10minutes|

Use class3 values despite total15. No Artificer5 Savant/9restorative features. Different effects can coexist where compatible; repeated copies do not automatically add together.
