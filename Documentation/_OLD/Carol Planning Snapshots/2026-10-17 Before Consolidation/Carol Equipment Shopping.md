## Crafting reference

2024 guide values: Common100gp to buy, or50gp/5workdays to craft; Uncommon400gp, or200gp/10workdays; Rare4000gp, or2000gp/50workdays. Base mundane item costs may be additional. These are guide values, not verified shop quotes. Crafting needs Arcana and the appropriate tools/proficiency, materials and, for spell-casting items, required prepared spells. Eight work hours/day; availability is DM-controlled. Alchemist's potion time benefit is not a blanket reduction for every magic item. Crafter's20% nonmagic purchase discount does not automatically discount magic items.

Sources: [2024 magic-item crafting/values](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/magic-items); [attunement](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/equipment#Attunement).
