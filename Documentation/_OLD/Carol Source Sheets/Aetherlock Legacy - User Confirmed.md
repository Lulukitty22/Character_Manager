# Aetherlock

Rifle, legendary (requires attunement by an Artificer)

A masterwork rifle with an oversized six-chambered cylinder and intricate arcane circuitry running through the frame. It was clearly built by a very seduced smith for personal use by their master. The weapon feels alive with latent magical potential.

# Weapon Statistics

Damage: 2d10 + INT piercing
Range: 40/120 ft.
Properties: Ammunition, Reload (6), Light
Weight: 3 lb.
One-handed.

# Core Feature – Arcane Chambers

The cylinder holds six chambers. During a short or long rest, you can prepare and load special rounds into them. You may have any mix of the following:

1. Infused Round

You bind one of your Infusions into the round (this counts against your maximum number of infused items). When you hit a creature or object with the round, the infusion is applied to the target as if it were wearing or holding the item for 10 minutes (or the infusion’s normal duration, whichever is shorter). You can prepare a number of Infused Rounds equal to your proficiency bonus.

2. Spellround

You cast a spell you know of 1st–5th level (casting time 1 action) into the round. The spell is stored without requiring concentration. When you fire the round and hit, the spell is cast targeting or centered on the creature/object you hit, using your spell attack bonus and save DC. If the spell normally requires a spell attack roll, it automatically hits the creature you shot. You can prepare a number of Spellrounds equal to your Artificer level ÷ 3 (rounded up). At level 15 this is 5 Spellrounds.

3. Arcane Round

A enhanced round that deals an extra 2d6 force damage on a hit. No special limit beyond the six-chamber capacity. The cylinder advances automatically after each shot. You can rotate it to any chamber as a bonus action.

# Unique Feature – Overclock

As a bonus action, you can overclock the revolver (uses equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest). Until the end of your turn you gain one of the following benefits:

Double Tap: The next attack you make fires two chambers at once (two attack rolls, or one attack that delivers both effects).

Precision Lock: The next attack is made with advantage, and if it is a Spellround or Infused Round the save DC increases by 2.

Rapid Cycle: You can fire the revolver twice this turn instead of once (each shot still uses a chamber).

# Legendary Feature – Resonant Cylinder

When you score a critical hit with Aetherlock, or when you use Overclock, you can immediately rotate the cylinder to any chamber of your choice as part of the same action (no bonus action required).

Further Investment Upgrades (These can be unlocked through downtime, rare materials, or by other means)

1. Higher Capacity Core

Maximum Spellround level increases to 6th level. You can prepare a number of Spellrounds equal to your Artificer level ÷ 2 (rounded up).

2. Twin-Linked Cylinder

Overclock’s Double Tap can now fire three chambers at once if you expend two uses of Overclock.

3. Living Circuitry

The weapon regains one expended use of Overclock whenever you finish a short rest. In addition, once per long rest you can cast one of your prepared Spellrounds without expending the round (the spell still fires from the barrel).

4. Masterwork Frame (Revolver variant option)

You may convert Aetherlock into a long barreled revolver. Damage becomes 1d12 + INT piercing. Range 80/240 ft. Gains +1 to attack rolls and gain an extra attack.
