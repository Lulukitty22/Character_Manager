His entire belief was the world was meant to be learned, that a vital requirement to be human is to understand and dissect the world around you into graspable terms.  These teachings rubbed off on the Original Elfnien, who studied and worked with her father. One year there was a plague outbreak, and Elfnien's father developed a cure using science and alchemy. It was deemed a miracle, however.. the higher state discovered this miracle. Her father was not a miracle worker, nor high in the social ladder or rich or of ANY court influence. He was also repeatedly seen praying, worshipping, and doing other near-arcane arts relating to the gods. They deemed him a witch, and burned him at the stake alive infront of his own daughter.   Being forced to watch her father burn alive broke her. She took that teachings of "disect the world" literally, and started using arcane magic, learning and dissecting the world in the most literal form, down to its atomic structure. She went rampant, learning and using skills that, at the time - and mostly still are, arcane magic to get what she wanted, her father back. She quite literally took the ash and remains of her father the next night and stored them for later.. however.. with time, the remains eventually dispersed and degraded too much to be of use.  She never got her Papa back, and still is trying to find a way to break the heavens for just another minute with her father.
 She's already learned how to break some laws of humanity, such as living forever and being reborn - however all at a severe cost. She's lived on her own in/near the remains of the village after she herself slaughtered the residents in a fit of rage and anger in hopes to equivalent exchange souls for a soul. Her mental state is effectively shattered after all this time, and her memories are all scattered and difficult or impossible to recover fully. She is distinctly separated from the modern times and language, so communication is difficult at best. She has no idea that magic is not forbidden anymore.

The Autoscores can hold a very, very limited amount of instructions and memories, so they are rather basic and almost can never afford emotions or memories that are not skill based or relevant to the task Elfnien created them for.   A common skillset in her Autoscore creation is to copy the adventure treasure hunter skill set from adventurers she kills. She adds on a memory and instructions such as: “Retrieve X” or “act as Maid” etc.   These golems require upkeep as they run on Alchemy, and similarly to Elfnien they burn memories in exchange for power/mana to operate.  however, for the most part the upkeep can be done by creating a specific Autoscore that their sole job is to.. collect.. more memories. Damage: 2d10 + INT piercing Range: 40/120 ft. Properties: Ammunition, Reload (6), Light Weight: 3 lb. One-handed.   
The cylinder holds six chambers. During a short or long rest, you can prepare and load special rounds into them. You may have any mix of the following:  
1. Infused Round
 You bind one of your Infusions into the round (this counts against your maximum number of infused items). When you hit a creature or object with the round, the infusion is applied to the target as if it were wearing or holding the item for 10 minutes (or the infusion’s normal duration, whichever is shorter). You can prepare a number of Infused Rounds equal to your proficiency bonus.  
2. Spellround
 You cast a spell you know of 1st–5th level (casting time 1 action) into the round. The spell is stored without requiring concentration. When you fire the round and hit, the spell is cast targeting or centered on the creature/object you hit, using your spell attack bonus and save DC. If the spell normally requires a spell attack roll, it automatically hits the creature you shot. You can prepare a number of Spellrounds equal to your Artificer level ÷ 3 (rounded up). At level 15 this is 5 Spellrounds.  
3. Arcane Round
 A enhanced round that deals an extra 2d6 force damage on a hit. No special limit beyond the six-chamber capacity. The cylinder advances automatically after each shot. You can rotate it to any chamber as a bonus action.  
As a bonus action, you can overclock the revolver (uses equal to your proficiency bonus per long rest). Until the end of your turn you gain one of the following benefits:  Double Tap: The next attack you make fires two chambers at once (two attack rolls, or one attack that delivers both effects).  Precision Lock: The next attack is made with advantage, and if it is a Spellround or Infused Round the save DC increases by 2.  Rapid Cycle: You can fire the revolver twice this turn instead of once (each shot still uses a chamber).
