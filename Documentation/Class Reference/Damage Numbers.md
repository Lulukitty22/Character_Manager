## Tome and double proficiency clarifications

2024 Tome of Clear Thought: study48hours within6days, **actual INT rises by2**, maximum30; then that book loses magic for100years. INT20→22 is the simple example. It does not grant free character-creation points, turn INT directly into30 or merely permit an otherwise empty higher score. The2014 version also raised INT by2 and explicitly raised its personal maximum by2; the2024 version directly specifies the increase up to30.

Expertise doubles PB on specified skill checks, not attack rolls or spellDC. Wizard2 Scholar already offers one qualifying proficient knowledge skill. At total15/PB5/INT6, selected Arcana Expertise would be d20+16; ordinary proficient Arcana d20+11; spell attack stays+11. Selecting that actual expertise skill remains open. Multiclassing two classes proficient with a weapon does not double its attack proficiency.

## Animate Objects, revised2024

Likely intended spell: **Animate Objects**, fifth-tier Transmutation, concentration up to1minute, not Telekinesis. It animates actual eligible nonmagical objects, with capacity equal to casting modifier6. Medium-or-smaller counts1; Large2; Huge3. Damage uses size categories, not pounds. Animated creatures attack immediately after Carol's turn; command uses a Bonus Action. Need reachable enemies and surviving constructs. Revised creatures have30ft speed, not an assumed flying swarm.

| Object selection | Capacity used | Fifth-tier average if all hit | Sixth-tier upcast average if all hit |
|---|---|---|---|
|6 Medium or smaller|6|33=6×(1d4+3)|48=6×(2d4+3)|
|3 Large|6|48=3×(2d6+3+INT6)|58.5=3×(3d6+3+INT6)|
|2 Huge|6|44=2×(2d12+3+INT6)|57=2×(3d12+3+INT6)|

Among these size mixes at fifth/sixth tier, three Large objects have the highest ordinary-hit damage, but require three actual Large objects and positioning. Six pocket-size objects are more portable but do less damage. Current spell increases each creature's dice when upcast; it does not add the old2014 fixed object counts. Evoker does not add+6: this spell is Transmutation, and creatures have their own attacks.

After casting, Carol can command the objects while using her later main actions for non-concentration spells. Examples after setup: Animate5 threeLarge48 + Evoker Cone5 42=90raw damage to an enemy caught by both; Animate5 48 + Disintegrate6 75=123raw if all object attacks hit and save fails. These combinations work for either subclass except the Evoker-specific Cone bonus. Concentration on Animate cannot coexist with self-maintained Guise.

Telekinesis5 targets one creature/object at a time and is control/manipulation; it does not provide a built-in weight-to-damage formula. Do not invent a damage table for throwing giant objects and label it published spell damage.

## Stronger setup comparison: Conjure Minor Elementals

Published2024 spell with official2025 errata: fourth-tier concentration, action to activate, extra2d8 on Carol's attack hits against targets within its15ft emanation. **Upcasting adds1d8 per tier above4**, not the original2d8. At sixth tier it adds4d8 (18average) per hit. Applies to weapon attacks and spell attacks; not to save-only spells or animated creatures' own attacks. No Evoker bonus on the Conjuration buff itself.

- Bladesinger's conditional True Strike whip + ordinary whip package:24+2×18=**60raw** after setup. Requires INT ordinary-weapon use independently of Guise, because Guise and CME both require concentration; full final Bladesong mechanics still need verification.
- Evoker casting fifth-slot Scorching Ray after sixth-slot CME:6rays×(2d6+4d8)+INT6 once=**156raw** if all hit. Targets must be within15ft at hit; stay beyond5ft from threatening enemies to avoid normal ranged-attack Disadvantage unless another feature addresses it. One setup action and two spell slots across different turns; not156 every round without spending ray slots.
- Same ray package for non-Evoker:150raw. Both subclasses can cast this concentration spell; Evoker's extra is6, not6on every ray. Thus high-damage combinations are not solely an Evoker feature.

This is a relevant high-damage standard-rules package, not an exhaustive maximum or a selected Carol loadout. Concentration loss, range and spending an action on setup are real tradeoffs in a six-turn fight. Guise/CME/Animate are alternative concentration choices, not simultaneous buffs.

## Accuracy-adjusted examples

Assume enemyAC18, no advantage/resistance, attack+11; normal-hit probability65%, critical5%, miss30%. Expected damage includes critical dice:

- Two INT whip attacks:12.15.
- True Strike whip + ordinary whip:17.4.
- Animate5 threeLarge:34.65; Animate6 threeLarge:42.525.
- Disintegrate against DEXsave+6/DC19:45, because it fails60% and takes0on success.
- CME6 + Scorching Ray5 Evoker: approximately118.5 if the+6 is applied to a successful ray (at least one ray hits with probability1−0.3^6); no disadvantage and targets in15ft. This counts setup as already completed, not averaged across the setup round.

These enemy statistics are examples, not observed campaign values. Raw all-hit values are not guaranteed actual DPR. No absolute cap on area spell encounter damage without a target count.
