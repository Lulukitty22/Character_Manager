## Is sixth tier worth keeping?

Yes for distinct capabilities; it is not mandatory for Carol's minimum goal. Fifth tier already supplies major area damage, control and creation. Sixth offers stronger single-target force damage, selected-target lightning, repeated line damage and specialized protection. The trade is actual Wizard11 access versus two levels invested in equipment features.

| Casting | Raw average per affected target | Shape / reliability |
|---|---|---|
| Fireball, third-tier slot |28 (8d6)|20-foot-radius; DEX half |
| Fireball, sixth-tier upcast |38.5 (11d6)|Same area; this is available from Wizard9 with a shared sixth-tier slot |
| Chain Lightning, sixth tier |45 (10d8)|Up to four chosen targets; DEX half |
| Disintegrate, sixth tier |75 (10d6+40)|One creature; failed DEX save required, otherwise no damage |
| Meteor Swarm, ninth tier |140 (40d6)|Large areas; DEX half; overlapping areas do not multiply one target's damage |

Sources: [2024 Fireball](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618887-fireball), [Chain Lightning](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2618960-chain-lightning), [Disintegrate](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619094-disintegrate), [Meteor Swarm](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619035-meteor-swarm). Averages are our arithmetic, before saves, resistance, accuracy or subclass bonuses; they are not damage per round predictions.

Four failed Chain Lightning saves sum to180 raw damage. Four failed saves against the sixth-slot Fireball sum to154; Fireball can catch more creatures but also allies. Enemy positions, saves and resistances determine the better choice. A higher tier does not guarantee the best horde spell.

## Intelligence and the gun

At INT22/total12: modifier+6; PB+4; base Wizard/Artificer DC=8+6+4=18; spell attack=6+4=+10. A plain INT check is+6, a proficient INT skill+10, Scholar Expertise+14 where eligible. None means every damage roll gains+6. Total14 changes PB to+5, DC19 and attack+11.

Aetherlock's given base damage is2d10+INT: raw average17 at INT22. Arcane Round adds2d6 for24. Two successful ordinary base hits would total34, but allowed attacks, costs, ammunition and Overclock interpretation remain open. No combined Double Tap/Rapid Cycle/Extra Attack stack is assumed. [Weapon issue file](../Issues/Aetherlock%20Issues.md).

2024 True Strike is a useful option to evaluate: one proficient weapon attack using the casting ability, with cantrip-scaled extra radiant damage. At character12 its extra2d6 would give24 average with the base gun, subject to weapon/spell eligibility. It consumes the spell action; it does not grant a second ordinary attack by itself. It is **not selected**, and Aetherlock's spell/weapon trigger interactions still need discussion. [Published True Strike](https://www.dndbeyond.com/spells/2619204-true-strike).

Cantrip scaling usually uses total character level, while class features use that class's level. Fire Bolt's scaling does not require Wizard11 specifically. Weapon Extra Attack, cantrip replacement and follow-up strikes are distinct features; read the selected versions before combining them.

## Preparing ahead: four different systems

| System | What it means for Carol |
|---|---|
| Prepared list | What she can cast now; does not prepay slots or store a cast |
| Sorcery Points | Conversion/modification currency; maximum and created-slot limits apply |
| Artificer stored spell | Item-based use at Artificer11; eligible Artificer spell and feature limits apply |
| Aetherlock Spellround | Granted custom preloaded cast; its own slot payment, capacity and discharge rules |

The gun already provides some of the desired pre-battle storage. That could reduce the need for Sorcerer. User now permits revising it; compare clear alternatives and discuss changes before choosing one. Generic crafting and Fabricate do not automatically produce free permanent magic items. Materials, tools, crafting rules and item plans remain separate requirements.
