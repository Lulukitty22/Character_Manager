## Wizard9 / Artificer3, total12

Suggested fourteen-spell Wizard preparation for a balanced trial:

| Purpose | Picks | Why / tradeoff |
|---|---|---|
| Defense |Shield, Mage Armor, Mirror Image|Reaction, armor base and duplicate defenses have different triggers; not three permanent AC bonuses |
| Mobility |Misty Step, Fly|Teleport versus sustained flight; Fly competes for concentration |
| Control |Web, Polymorph, Wall of Force|Different target/area options; all compete for concentration |
| Damage |Magic Missile, Fireball, Cone of Cold|Reliable small burst, distant area, close cone; sixth-slot upcasting available |
| Countermagic |Counterspell, Dispel Magic|Reaction interruption versus removing ongoing magic;2024 Counterspell differs from old version |
| Engineering |Fabricate|Practical material fabrication; tool proficiency still matters for skilled goods |

Fourteen entries match Wizard9's core allowance. Detect Magic and Find Familiar are useful book-ritual candidates outside that ordinary prepared list under Wizard's ritual feature; house-policy book eligibility remains to be defined. [Wizard progression/list](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes#Wizard), [spell definitions](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/spell-descriptions).

Swap options, rather than silently preparing everything: Creation for temporary material; Legend Lore for research; a different defensive/control spell for the expected fight. Keep elemental spells if they express Carol better than maximizing raw damage.

Artificer3 adds introductory item replication, tools and one specialty's opening kit. Alchemist contributes elixirs, Artillerist a cannon, Battle Smith a defender/weapon package; none at3 has its5th-level specialty upgrade or6th-level item conversion. Aetherlock stays its granted custom item. New class resources do not replace its own rounds.

## Pure-class counterexamples

**Wizard12:** the same sixth-tier spell choices plus its12th-level feat; Scholar and flexible preparation remain. Alchemy/tool use can still come from background/custom grants, but no Artificer item engine is implied. Its gun attunement prerequisite needs an explicit exception if no Artificer level is retained.

**Sorcerer12:** sixth-tier combat magic, twelve-point pool, restoration and origin features. A sixteen-spell trial can use Shield, Mage Armor, Magic Missile, Web, Mirror Image, Misty Step, Fireball, Counterspell, Dispel Magic, Fly, Polymorph, Cone of Cold, Animate Objects, Disintegrate, Chain Lightning and Globe of Invulnerability. This gives plenty of combat capability but trades away Fabricate, Wall of Force, Wizard book rituals and Scholar. Standard CHA remains a separate problem for Carol; INT homebrew would not restore those missing capabilities.

**Artificer12:** third-tier class spells, three specialty checkpoints, item conversion, Flash of Genius and Spell-Storing Item. Good equipment/party support; fails the current fifth-tier-spell minimum. Its third-tier spell access arrives four class levels later than Wizard/Sorcerer. Final2025 spell/preparation details remain source-gated.

## Maximum-level identities

| Pure20 | What the finished character emphasizes | Carol cost |
|---|---|---|
|Wizard|Ninth-tier magic, book utility, expertise, repeatable low-tier and free signature casting|Crafting engine remains external; ordinary Wizard does not confer Sorcery Points |
|Sorcerer|Ninth-tier combat/control, twenty points, flexible spell modification and full origin|Less Wizard-specific research/creation; no item engine; CHA versus INT decision |
|Artificer|Fifth-tier magic, full specialty, broad item use, stored spells and advanced craft support|No class sixth-tier-or-higher magic; late fifth-tier unlock at17 |

Class theme is not the same as maximum raw spell damage. The engineering choice should earn usable crafting, tools, replicated items and resource rules in the actual sheet—not just another flavor tag.
