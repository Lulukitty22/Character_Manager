# Sorcerer

Sorcerer supplies full spell progression plus conversion and spell modification. The table uses2024 core; **CHA remains standard**. INT-based casting is only a proposed Carol house rule, not selected. Using spellbooks for Sorcerer is also table policy and does not itself grant Wizard features. [2024 changes](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1769-2024-sorcerer-vs-2014-sorcerer-whats-new).

*Published base counts, excluding subclass grants. Standard Sorcerer spell replacements occur on class level gain; it is not Wizard's flexible rest preparation. Your broader availability/equipping policy may override this, and needs to be recorded explicitly. Standard foundation: d6, CHA casting, CON/CHA starting saves; multiclassing does not grant those saves. [Published class](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes#Sorcerer).

## Why levels matter beyond slots

- **1:** Innate Sorcery temporarily improves Sorcerer spellDC and spell attacks, not every attack/check on the character.
- **2:** conversion and two Metamagic choices; pool2. This is a small pool, not unrestricted currency for all existing high slots.
- **5:** limited Short Rest point recovery; budget floor(SorcererLevel/2), once per Long Rest.
- **7:** additional Innate activation via points, and two Metamagic options per cast during Innate.
- **10/17:** more Metamagic choices.
- **20:** free Metamagic option each turn during Innate; ninth-tier spells already at17.

[Feature overview](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1769-2024-sorcerer-vs-2014-sorcerer-whats-new).

No sixth-or-higher slots can be created through Font. Converting a slot yields points equal to its tier but never bypasses the pool maximum; excess/capping behavior should be explicit in UI. Created slots expire at Long Rest. Adept points are a separate restricted source, not an excuse to grant Font. [2024 core conversion rules](https://www.dndbeyond.com/sources/dnd/br-2024/character-classes#Sorcerer).

## Carol-specific tradeoff

Pure20 grants ninth-tier Sorcerer spells, points20, Incarnate/Apotheosis and an origin's progression. It loses Wizard-only spells/rituals/Scholar and Artificer crafting unless separately granted. INT homebrew fixes the casting score, not those omissions. Sorcerer2 in a triple class buys a small conversion/Metamagic package at the cost of two levels elsewhere; modern Artificer6 may better match item-oriented resource reuse. Quandrix is playtest/adaptation, never an assumed published2024 origin.
