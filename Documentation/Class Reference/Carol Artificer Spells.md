## Rules boundaries

- Cure Wounds 1 heals 2d8 +6, mean 15. Healing Word 1 heals 2d4 +6, mean 11, as a Bonus Action at 60 ft. Each gains two healing dice per higher slot. Artificer 3 has no Alchemical Savant.
- Ray of Sickness 1 uses a +11 ranged spell attack, 2d8 Poison, and Poisoned until the end of Carol's next turn on hit, without another CON save under the recorded 2024 text. It gains 1d8 per higher slot. It has no Wizard Evoker bonus; immunities apply.
- True Strike uses one weapon attack with casting ability and its applicable +2d6 Radiant at total level 15. No Extra Attack, doubled INT or Evoker bonus. The [selected weapon rules](Aetherlock%20Engineered%20Rounds.md) govern its separate firing-time rider; it is not a stored payload or burst-wide cast.
- Faerie Fire requires concentration and a DEX save in its 20 ft cube. Attack Advantage requires seeing the affected target. It competes with Guise, Web, Animate Objects and Wall of Force.
- Identify takes one minute, or eleven as a ritual under the recorded ritual rules, and needs its reusable 100 gp pearl, which is owned. Message is private 120 ft communication without concentration. Mending takes one minute, repairs an eligible one-foot break and does not restore lost magic.
- Actual Artificer spell access remains level 1. Higher shared slots upcast eligible spells; they do not unlock higher Artificer tiers. Book placement does not change origin or create another slot pool.

Detect Magic is selected as an Artificer level 1 entry, replacing the earlier inferred exclusion for party overlap. It uses concentration and can be cast as a ritual under the campaign readiness rules; ritual casting avoids a slot, not concentration. Guidance/Spare the Dying were omitted for reported Cleric coverage and Light was eliminated. Shocking Grasp is now selected as a Wizard cantrip; the old statement that it was unselected is superseded.

Other revised or older-supplement cantrips discussed are alternatives, not grants. Catalogue inclusion alone does not prove final revised class access; preserve source/version. The exact class and elixir working limits are recorded in [Items and Elixirs](Carol%20Artificer%20Items%20and%20Elixirs.md), rather than described here as wholly unavailable.
