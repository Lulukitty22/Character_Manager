**Artisan/Crafter is the first candidate.** Three extra artisan proficiencies overlap Manifold; free temporary mundane gear overlaps Artificer Tinker's Magic (not every item identical, and separate use pools). Crafter still gives20% off nonmagical purchases and native tool proficiency without an item. No blanket magic-item or crafting-material discount. [Published Crafter](https://roll20.net/compendium/dnd5e/Feats%3ACrafter?expansion=32231&iframe=true).

**Published Sage is an alternative**, keeping scholar/crafter roleplay possible while supplying Magic Initiate(Wizard): two extra Wizard cantrips and one chosen first-level spell with one free cast/Long Rest. Choosing already-selected Mage Armor would add a free daily casting source, not duplicate its book entry. Sage grants Arcana/History and Calligrapher proficiency; Wizard skill allocation and background stat bonuses must be rebuilt rather than stacking with Artisan. ## What Manifold does not replace

- War Caster: concentration support and casting benefits; unrelated to artisan proficiency.
- Skill Expert / Scholar: skill Expertise, not a duplicate native tool grant. Retain for Arcana/Investigation unless user separately changes priorities.
- Human Alert: initiative support; independent of tools.
- Alchemist: elixirs and potion-crafting benefit, plus Herbalism (not an artisan tool). Manifold alone does not supply this subclass.
