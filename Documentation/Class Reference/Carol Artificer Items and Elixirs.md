# Carol Artificer Items and Elixirs

Updated 2026-10-18. Working reference for Alchemist Artificer 3. The official full chapter remained gated in prior research. Final-book-labelled Wikidot transcriptions identify Forge of the Artificer and are distinct from UA. Treat exact details as secondary evidence; official previews corroborate the feature structure. No fresh verification is claimed here.

## Grants and selected items

The revised multiclass transcription grants Tinker's Tools proficiency and one Artificer skill. Nature is the selected skill proficiency (+11). Human Medicine and Wizard Insight are selected. Sage replaced Artisan/Crafter, so no Crafter tool choices remain.

At Artificer 3, the working limit is four known plans and two active creations, using class level rather than total level 15. Creations are produced after a Long Rest; exceeding the active limit replaces the oldest. They do not all expire each Long Rest like elixirs. No level 6 conversion/recharge feature is available.

Selected plans: Wand of the War Mage +1, Manifold Tool, Goggles of Night and Alchemy Jug. Usual active replicas are Goggles and Jug. Wand and Manifold are permanent prior acquisitions outside replica capacity; their plans remain known without adding active copies. The gifted rifle and two owned Bags of Holding are also separate property. Historical purchase/crafting details and gold ledger are unspecified.

| Selected item | Benefit / limitation |
|---|---|
| Wand +1 | Held and attuned: applicable spell attacks +1 and ignore Half Cover. Base +11 becomes +12; no DC or gun-aim bonus. |
| Manifold | Attuned; a Magic action transforms it into artisan tools, granting proficiency while used. No simultaneous kits, whole workshop or Herbalism/Thieves' Tools. Native proficiency does not stack another PB. |
| Alchemy Jug | One selected listed liquid within daily limits. No arbitrary potions, spell components or ammunition; no attunement. |
| Goggles of Night | Worn: 60 ft Darkvision for Human Carol; no attunement. |

Aetherlock, Wand and Manifold occupy all three attunements. Review hands/handling before assuming held-item bonuses during a Two-Handed rifle attack. [Equipment Shopping](Carol%20Equipment%20Shopping.md) distinguishes selected gear from candidates.

## Experimental Elixirs

The working class-3 transcription grants two random elixirs after a Long Rest while holding Alchemist's Supplies. Roll d6; a 6 permits choosing the effect. A Bonus Action drinks/administers one within 5 ft. A Magic action and a spell slot create one chosen extra elixir; higher slots do not automatically increase quantity or strength.

Unused elixirs vanish at the next Long Rest. No concentration requirement is stated. Keep this class pool separate from crafted permanent potions and non-restoring gun ammunition. Alchemist also halves potion crafting time; this is not a universal discount for every item or ammunition recipe.

| Effect | Value at Carol's Artificer 3 |
|---|---|
| Healing | 2d8 +6 HP, mean 15 |
| Swiftness | Speed +10 ft for one hour |
| Resilience | AC +1 for ten minutes |
| Boldness | +1d4 to attacks/saves for one minute |
| Flight | Fly 10 ft for ten minutes |

Use class-3 values, not total level 15. No Artificer 5 Alchemical Savant or level 9 restorative features apply. Different compatible effects may coexist; repeated copies do not automatically stack.

Healing Word and Ray of Sickness at Alchemist 3 agree with the older selected fallback and the final-book-labelled transcription. Preserve the secondary provenance while using revised spell definitions. Mandatory class grants are not extra item-plan choices.
