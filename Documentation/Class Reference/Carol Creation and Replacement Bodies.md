# Carol Creation and Replacement Bodies

Updated 2026-10-18. Carol uses 2024 Human with Alert; Reborn mechanics are retired. [Build Sources](Carol%20Build%20Sources.md) records Medicine as the Human skill and INT 22 including Indomitable. Her alchemical appearance remains flavor, without an inferred Construct type or extra physiology.

## Current decisions

Replacement bodies are setpiece/lore, not a usable extra-life system. Current Carol has one life. Memories and progression do not automatically transfer to a backup. A later body would be roughly level 8; that is narrative guidance, not an approved automated reset mechanic.

Do not add automatic respawn, full-level restoration, resurrection charges or active clone party members. This does not prohibit ordinary resurrection spells; that separate campaign policy was not answered. Any active Autoscore mechanics require their own discussion.

The [original Discord source](../_OLD/Carol%20Source%20Sheets/DISCORD%20CAROL%20ELFNEIN%20SHEET.txt), especially lines 122 and 172–173, records historical intent. It does not restore old powers or the retired memory-resource system.

## Historical intent

- Replacement bodies: inactive copies of Carol, periodically updated with selected memories; next copy activates on death. Some can activate early as independent people, not a hive mind. Old text calls the lineage a custom Reborn/Ralis mixture.
- Autoscores: artificial servants that maintain replacement bodies and perform limited assigned tasks. Old source describes transferred soul/memory fragments, upkeep and memory consumption.
- Source line122 describes making life by placing an existing being into an artificial vessel; not creation of a new soul from nothing.
