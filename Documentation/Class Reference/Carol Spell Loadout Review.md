Mind Sliver and Shocking Grasp are selected. At total level 15, Mind Sliver deals 3d6 Psychic on a failed INT save and penalizes the next save by 1d4 within its timing window. Shocking Grasp deals 3d8 Lightning plus applicable Wizard Evocation +6, and prevents Opportunity Attacks only on a hit; do not restore legacy all-Reaction denial or metal-armor Advantage.

Detect Magic is selected in Workshop/Alchemy as an Artificer entry. Do not silently add Polymorph or another unselected spell.

## Rules and calculation boundaries

- Use revised 2024 spell definitions, with Guise explicitly retained from Tasha's. Source confidence is recorded in [the sheet](../Character%20Sheets/Carol%20Elfnein.md) and [Build Sources](Carol%20Build%20Sources.md).
- Empowered Evocation adds +6 to one eligible Wizard Evocation damage roll per casting, not every ray or every school. Disintegrate and Mind Sliver do not receive it. Means are not guaranteed DPR.
- Shield adds +5 AC until the relevant next-turn boundary. Repeated casting does not stack that bonus. Mirror Image is separate interception, not increased AC; it does not protect against save-only effects. Shield and Counterspell compete for the Reaction.
- Mage Armor is a non-concentration unarmored base; robes add no armor bonus. Guise, Web, Wall of Force and Animate Objects are alternative concentration choices.
- Preserve the 2024 slot-per-turn casting restriction, normal actions, triggers and components. A higher shared slot does not grant a higher-level class spell.
- Animate Objects needs actual eligible objects, commands, reach, survival and concentration. Fabricate needs raw materials, time and appropriate proficiency for skilled work; it does not create magic items.
- Alchemist potion/elixir features and source limits belong in [Items and Elixirs](Carol%20Artificer%20Items%20and%20Elixirs.md). Aetherlock recipes are separate campaign mechanics.

The full official Artificer chapter remained gated in prior research. The final-book-labelled Alchemist transcription agrees with Healing Word/Ray of Sickness, but is secondary confirmation. Do not describe it as direct full-book verification or grant higher-level class features at Artificer 3.
