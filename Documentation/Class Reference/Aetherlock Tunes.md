# Aetherlock Tunes

**Superseded proposals:** user rejected these three specific tunes. Working design is now [Engineered Rounds](Aetherlock%20Engineered%20Rounds.md): six unrestricted chambers, up to six Spellrounds at once, prepaid consumable ammo, possible configurable fuzes. Earlier proposed five-total-level, one-Spellround and per-rest module caps were never approved; do not carry them into the new design. Keep this file as comparison history.

User permits revising the original gun because its designer did not know the class mechanics, including adding/changing rounds and clarifying/reworking magical rounds. Compare three proposals with Carol's possible builds and ask frequent short questions. These are **design directions, not complete selected rules**; no weapon/character data changed. Six chambers remain a provisional starting point. The original remains archived in [Discord Source](../_OLD/Carol%20Source%20Sheets/DISCORD%20CAROL%20ELFNEIN%20SHEET.txt); unresolved rules are indexed in [Aetherlock Issues](../Issues/Aetherlock%20Issues.md).

## Shared constraints to propose, not assume

- One weapon attack ability and proficiency rule; displayed attack and damage formulas separately.
- Explicit load/reload/rotate/fire actions, chamber state and what a miss consumes.
- Spell eligibility by actual class/item grant, not highest shared slot.
- Loading pays an actual slot and required materials once; stored casting cannot duplicate that paid cast freely.
- An explicit concentration rule after release; storage alone does not grant multiple simultaneous effects.
- Choose either separate weapon/spell resolution or a clear replacement attack procedure; no undefined automatic hits/critical multiplication.
- Extra Attack, Overclock and burst modes share a defined attack budget; no accidental stacking.
- One cap/reset/source for every ammunition or charge pool; no unlimited stock from repeated rests.

These constraints address ambiguity. Numeric strength is a separate comparison: original2d10+INT averages17 before special rounds; changing that base is not decided. Different tuned builds should be compared at equal total levels where possible, with total15 benefits priced separately from total12/14.

## First mechanical draft comparison — remaining numbers are proposals

Common approved rules: Spellrounds replace ordinary gun damage; the gun attack replaces a spell attack; normal saves and spell damage rolls remain; Carol handles concentration after firing. Six chambers and original ordinary2d10+INT damage are provisional carryovers. Keep the attack ability, proficiency, miss/area/multi-ray/critical rules and reload action open until answered.

Draft A's capacity borrows a familiar storage benchmark, **not** all rules of a Ring of Spell Storing. Five stored levels could be one Fireball plus two first-tier spells, or one Cone of Cold; the six physical chambers do not grant six free fifth-tier casts. Draft B's module limit and Draft C's firing/Metamagic eligibility are proposed choices, not existing approved gun rules. [Published ring benchmark](https://www.dndbeyond.com/magic-items/4730-ring-of-spell-storing).

Same simple damage case: one hit with a Fireball Spellround uses Fireball's damage/save, never17 extra ordinary gun damage. A regular base shot averages17 at INT22 if that damage is retained. A force-module shot's extra damage remains to be selected. Do not compare C's extra class levels as if they were free weapon improvements.

Recommendation for the next comparison, not final selection: use A as the spell-storage baseline and B as the crafting baseline; test C only if actual Metamagic still matters after the gun's preparation role is clear. Compare each with the same Wizard9 core loadout and a separately labeled same-total14 build. Next questions: miss consumption and area targeting, then storage/modules and attunement. Overclock/upgrades come after the basic shot works.
