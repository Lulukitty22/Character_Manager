## Weapon assumptions and results

Use two ordinary shortswords (each1d6, Light/Finesse), proficiency, an actual permission to use INT for ordinary weapon attack/damage, and Bladesinger's cantrip replacement for the cantrip rows. INT-guided Aetherlock permission does not extend to swords. Guise is one possible INT-weapon enabler, but costs a sixth-tier slot, Bonus Action and concentration; it cannot coexist with Animate Objects. Final2025 Bladesinger text remains to verify before importing; the official published older errata verifies the one-cantrip replacement model. Without INT permission, ordinary attacks use STR/DEX; historical DEX16 is unapproved and not silently substituted here.

Base Light bonus attack does not add positive ability modifier. Two-Weapon Fighting fighting style would add6 to each legal row:28.5,35.5,37.5 initial and51 with BB movement. This is a separate feature/feat prerequisite, not granted simply by holding two weapons or selecting Dual Wielder. Wizard/Alchemist does not automatically get it. Bladesong/Guise activation uses the Bonus Action, removing the ordinary Light bonus attack on that activation turn (subtract3.5 from base table).

Whip is1d4/Finesse/Reach, not Light. Do not pretend these are Light whips or wield two swords plus a third whip simultaneously. A mixed Light+whip build needs separate rules/feat verification and recalculation. BB requires target within5ft even with a Reach weapon; forced movement does not trigger its later damage. True Strike is a different cantrip, not a buff stacked onto BB. Cantrip scaling uses character level15 here (2d6 TS,2d8 initial/3d8 movement BB); Wizard12 alone has the same cantrip dice.

Nick mastery moves the Light bonus attack into the Attack action, freeing the Bonus Action; it alone does not create another attack. Nick+revised Dual Wielder may support a separate fourth attack with further investment, but final feat text/access must be verified before giving Carol that package. No such mastery/style/feat silently granted and no forum interpretation imported as an approved build.

## Sorcerer3 and damage type

Transmuted Spell costs1SP and can change among Acid/Cold/Fire/Lightning/Poison/Thunder. All types still give36 normal or42 with Evoker at slot5 against an ordinary failed-save target. No intrinsic acid corrosion, fire burning, shock stun or second-round tick is added. Cone is instantaneous; average combined initial+later damage is still36/42. Resistance to chosen type gives18/21; vulnerability72/84; immunity0. These are target-specific, not inherent rankings. School remains Evocation, so a Wizard-origin Cone retains its eligible Evoker bonus after transmutation. Sorcerer3 alone cannot prepare Cone; metamagic can modify the Wizard casting. Innate Sorcery's Sorcerer-spell-specific DC bonus does not automatically apply to this Wizard casting.

Sorcerer3 has3SP at a time and cannot manufacture fifth-tier slots through Flexible Casting (its creation ceiling is tier2). A high shared slot does not unlock higher Sorcerer spells. Do not use INT for Sorcerer spellcasting without a separate approved homebrew; none needed to transmute a Wizard spell.
