## Damage interpretation and comparison

Earlier36/42 Cone and16.5/22.5 Fire Bolt values were **Carol's outgoing damage to enemies**, not self-damage. At Wizard12, Evoker features impose no self-damage penalty. Overchannel is Wizard14: first use per Long Rest is safe; subsequent uses inflict escalating self-damage. It is absent from the current Wizard12 build and must not be mixed into its damage table.

Example at INT20: Cone36 versus Evoker41; Fire Bolt16.5 versus21.5; two Cones plus four Fire Bolts raw138 versus168. INT22 example138 versus174 remains conditional. Totals are per affected target, before accuracy/saves, with every action used for those spells. Evoker's half-damage-on-cantrip-miss improves reliability beyond these ordinary-hit totals. No implicit extra damage to Disintegrate (Transmutation), no bonus on every ray, no automatic transfer to custom stored rounds.

| Specialty | Benefit for Carol | What she gives up by choosing it |
|---|---|---|
|Evoker|More direct Evocation damage, reliable damage cantrips, safer area damage around selected allies|Bladesong and attack/cantrip package; Abjurer's ward and specialized countermagic |
|Bladesinger|Weapon/cantrip turns and conditional personal defenses|Evoker's direct spell-damage bonuses and reliable cantrip misses; Guise overlaps some weapon benefits |
|Abjurer|Ward and countermagic; keeps basic damaging spells|Extra offensive subclass damage/weapon features; currently less relevant when Carol already stays conscious |

Given short fights and leftover slots, prioritize effective casts per turn over cantrip endurance alone. Use high-tier spell options rather than assuming four filler cantrips is her optimal rotation. Do not spend multiple early actions buffing without comparing their lost damage. Defensive reactions remain useful without changing her whole subclass to Abjurer.

## Transformation/buff candidates

Guise6 is the closest intended Faust form: flight/defense/weapon adaptation while retaining casting; full benefit definition still source-gated. Tenser6 favors weapon combat and disables casting, so poor fit for Carol's stated caster role. Neither requires Bladesinger. Published revised alternatives: Fly3 for flight without a full form; Haste3 for speed/AC/limited extra action with an ending drawback; Greater Invisibility4 for fighting/casting while invisible; Alter Self2 for physical disguise/aquatic/natural-weapon options. These options require concentration, so they are alternatives to Guise, not a pile of simultaneously self-maintained buffs. Polymorph4 changes into a Beast and prevents ordinary casting in that form; poor fit for preserving her caster role. Shapechange9 is a future transformation, not available at Wizard12.
