# Carol Build Walkthrough

Updated 2026-10-18. Carol's accepted build is **Evoker Wizard 12 / Alchemist Artificer 3**, total level 15, INT 22. The former Wizard 9 / Alchemist 3 walkthrough and slot-funded cartridge recipe are superseded. This overview explains the current build; [Carol's sheet](../Character%20Sheets/Carol%20Elfnein.md) owns its details.

Wizard supplies actual spell access through level 6, Evoker features and most casting. Artificer/Alchemist supplies its early spellcasting, tool and item-making package, elixirs and related grants. Shared caster level is 14, with slots 4/3/3/3/2/1/1; the seventh-level slot supports upcasting rather than actual seventh-level spell access.

Human and Sage supply Alert, Medicine, knowledge/tool proficiencies and Magic Initiate (Wizard). War Caster and Skill Expert support concentration and skills. Indomitable is the explicit DM-granted +2 INT already included in 22. See [Build Sources](Carol%20Build%20Sources.md) for the full tree.

Carol uses three permanent Enduring Spellbooks under the campaign's 16-entry/all-eligible-ready rule. They contain 22 leveled spells and 10 cantrips. Mage Armor has one free level 1 Magic Initiate cast per Long Rest; ordinary extra casts use resources.

## Aetherlock in play

Aetherlock is a custom Two-Handed rifle with six chambers and a separate magical charge canister. Physical rounds and charges are different supplies. Each permitted attack can discharge a Basic/Arcane burst or a single Spellround/HE-FRAG projectile under the selected weapon rules. Guise's Attack action and fresh True Strike's one enhanced attack are separate action options.

A Spellround contains one actual scribed scroll mounted in a Basic Round. Scribing and mounting are distinct paid jobs; the old recipe requiring a payload slot is superseded. The scroll retains donor formulas and applicable statistics, rolled when released. Firing consumes ammunition and charges, without another payload slot payment. Normal saves, attacks and target restrictions still apply unless an explicit campaign exception changes them.

Physical ammunition loading is free under the selected weapon rule. Replacing a charge canister costs a Bonus Action and immediately locks the rifle until the start of the second subsequent own turn. Shield delivery has its own Reaction exception. See [Aetherlock Engineered Rounds](Aetherlock%20Engineered%20Rounds.md) and [Costs and Damage](Aetherlock%20Costs%20and%20Damage.md) for exact rules.
