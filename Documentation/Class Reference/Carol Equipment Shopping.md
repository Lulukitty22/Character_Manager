# Carol Equipment Shopping

Current equipment decisions, consolidated 2026-10-17. [Sheet](../Character%20Sheets/Carol%20Elfnein.md) owns the playable inventory; this file distinguishes approved items from shopping candidates. Prior proposal text is archived. No invented current gold debit or downtime ledger.

## Selected

- **Permanent:** Wand of the War Mage+1 and Manifold Tool, acquired during earlier downtime. Exact purchase-versus-crafting history unspecified; neither consumes class replication capacity.
- **Plans:** Wand+1, Manifold, Goggles of Night, Alchemy Jug. Usual active replicas **Goggles+Jug**, two total. Gifted Aetherlock and two separately owned Bags of Holding are not automatically replicas.
- **Attunement3/3:** Aetherlock (Artificer), Wand, Manifold. Goggles/Jug/books need none. Owning an item does not mean every held-item bonus is simultaneously active.
- **Books:** Combat, Cantrips, Workshop/Alchemy are permanent Enduring Spellbooks; same existing contents/order/16-entry campaign capacity. Fire/water/aging protection for book and writing, not blanket indestructibility or combat buffs.
- **Physical tools:** Alchemist's Supplies, Herbalism Kit, Tinker's Tools, Calligrapher's Supplies. Ownership separate from native/conditional proficiency.
- **Spell gear:** component pouch, Identify100 gp pearl and Guise500 gp engraved planar object, both reusable. The latter is owned, no longer pending; cosmetic form open.
- **Other:** robes; four Potions of Healing; ink/paper, rope, bedroll, waterskin, seven days' rations; two separate Bags of Holding. No bag nesting/contents assigned.
- **Ammunition34:**18 Basic Rounds,6Arcane,3ConeCold5,3HE-FRAG,2Shield1,2CureWounds1. Only six loaded at once. See [weapon](Aetherlock%20Engineered%20Rounds.md) for selected recipes and open supply costs.

Selected charge supply: five filled reusable 12-charge canisters, one installed and four spare, for 60 charges. Current empty refill is 12 gp reagents, one hour with Tinker's Tools and one first-level spell slot. The proposed 120 gp reagent cost remains unselected. Shells are retained; rests do not refill them automatically. Historical fills do not debit current resources.
