# Subclass Paths

> **Historical research.** The comparisons and pending-choice language below describe an earlier design stage. They do not reopen Carol's accepted Evoker Wizard 12 / Alchemist Artificer 3 build, Human/Sage grants or spell package. Use the [current sheet](../Character%20Sheets/Carol%20Elfnein.md) and [Build Sources](Carol%20Build%20Sources.md) for selected values, and [Campaigns and Shared Data](../Systems/Campaigns%20and%20Shared%20Data.md) to resume current work. Calculations here retain their stated assumptions and verification limits.

## Historical discussion

Choose a published path before importing its rules. Wizard and Sorcerer checkpoints are 3/6/10/14 and 3/6/14/18 respectively; Artificer specialty checkpoints are 3/5/9/15. These are comparison pointers, not complete replacement rule text. Level0–20 core tables are in the three class files. Exact newer specialty limits and spell grants remain open in [Source Verification](Source%20Verification.md).

## Wizard candidates

| Path | Why consider it for Carol | What higher Wizard levels buy |
|---|---|---|
| Bladesinger, published2025 | Closest continuation of her weapon/transformation concept |3 training/Bladesong;6 weapon/cantrip combination;10 defensive slot use;14 revised follow-up weapon strike |
| Evoker,2024 | Elemental combat rather than engineering |3 cantrip reliability;6 safer area damage around allies;10 INT damage feature;14 overchannel |
| Abjurer,2024 | Protection and magical defenses | Ward progression and later protective/countermagic checkpoints; full selected text needed |
| Diviner,2024 | Planned outcomes and analysis | Predictive roll replacement and higher divination features; not a general INT bonus |
| Illusionist,2024 | Deception, simulated constructs and battlefield tricks | Illusion/summoning progression; illusions are not permanent crafted equipment |
| Transmutation, older rules | Strong alchemist theme | Older stone/transformation mechanics; edition adaptation required, not a2024 core replacement |

[Published2025 Bladesinger overview](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2082-weave-your-tale-in-the-forgotten-realms-with-8), [2024 Wizard paths](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1753-2024-wizard-vs-2014-wizard-whats-new), [older Bladesinger comparison](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1048-wizard-101-bladesinging-from-tashas-cauldron-of).

At Wizard9 Carol reaches the level6 subclass feature, not the10 or14 features. Keeping Faust's transformation is a separate custom-item decision: it must not silently grant those missing checkpoints. Armor training from Artificer does not settle Bladesong's equipment restrictions. The published2025 Song of Victory is different from the old INT-to-weapon-damage version; do not stack both.

## Artificer candidates

| Specialty | Main role |3 →5 →9 →15 feature pointers | Carol fit / conflict to resolve |
|---|---|---|---|
| Alchemist | Potions, healing and elemental support | Elixirs → Alchemical Savant → Restorative Reagents → Chemical Mastery | Strongest potion identity; elixirs and gun rounds need separate rules |
| Artillerist | Ranged magical engineering | Cannon → Arcane Firearm → Explosive Cannon → Fortified Position | Strong gun theme; cannon commands compete with gun bonus actions |
| Battle Smith | Magic weapons and a companion | Battle Ready/defender → Extra Attack → Arcane Jolt → Improved Defender | Clear weapon/construct package; defender is not automatically an Autoscorer army |
| Armorer | Engineered wearable combat gear | Arcane Armor → Extra Attack → Improved Armorer → Perfected Armor | Useful if transformation becomes equipment; Bladesong restrictions matter |
| Cartographer | Maps, support and teleportation | Atlas → Guided Precision → Ingenious Movement → Superior Atlas | Strong travel utility; weaker match for her chemistry identity |

[Published five-specialty overview](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2109-forge-your-story-with-5-inventive-artificer). Names/milestones are indexing pointers; exact final dice, uses, action clauses and always-prepared lists must be checked before data import. No UA values are approved by this table.

Artillerist's Arcane Firearm bonus is for eligible **Artificer spells cast through it**, not every Wizard spell or ordinary gun hit. Battle Smith's INT weapon use requires its feature conditions. Aetherlock's own INT damage remains granted regardless of theme; its attack ability is an open weapon rule.

For comparison: Artificer3 buys the specialty's opening package;5 its next combat/support package;6 the class's item conversion;9 the third specialty checkpoint;11 stored third-tier spells;15 the final specialty checkpoint. Selecting Alchemist3 does not imply possession of its higher healing/damage features.

## Sorcerer candidates

| Origin | Main reason to compare | Caution |
|---|---|---|
| Draconic,2024 | Durability and elemental specialization | Applies its own element/feature conditions, not blanket INT damage |
| Aberrant,2024 | Mental communication and unusual spell access | Different identity; subclass grants do not make it a Wizard spellbook |
| Clockwork,2024 | Order, consistency and defensive support | Thematic fit for precision, not an item crafting engine |
| Wild Magic,2024 | Unstable magical output | Adds unpredictability rather than solving ammunition/crafting rules |
| Spellfire, published2025 | Point-driven magical bursts/protection | Final costs and interactions need published text |
| Mage of Quandrix,2021 UA | Historical probability/manipulation concept | Playtest, not a published Sorcerer origin; requires explicit adaptation |

[2024 Sorcerer paths](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/1769-2024-sorcerer-vs-2014-sorcerer-whats-new), [published Spellfire](https://www.dndbeyond.com/posts/2082-weave-your-tale-in-the-forgotten-realms-with-8), [Quandrix playtest](https://media.wizards.com/2021/dnd/downloads/UA2021_06_08_MagesStrixhaven.pdf).

Sorcerer2 already supplies Font and Metamagic but no2024 origin. Full Sorcerer20 supplies every origin checkpoint; this still does not supply Artificer crafting or Wizard-only spells. Changing CHA to INT would be a separate house rule, with multiclass prerequisites and spell origins addressed explicitly.
