## Tools and contextual checks

Native proficiencies are Calligrapher's Supplies, Tinker's Tools, Alchemist's Supplies and Herbalism Kit. Their physical kits are owned. The old native Glassblower, Smith, Mason and Potter grants were removed with Artisan/Crafter. Attuned Manifold can supply artisan-tool forms and proficiency while used; it does not supply Herbalism or Thieves' Tools, a complete workshop or extra PB.

When the DM calls for a relevant skill check and rules that proficient tool use also applies, the skill/tool rule may grant Advantage. Example: INT Investigation +16 with applicable Manifold Mason use, with no additional +5 or second Expertise. A plain tool check instead uses its applicable ability and proficiency; Investigation Expertise does not apply to every tool action.

Prior tool research recorded Smith and Mason as STR checks (+4 proficient for Carol), Tinker/Weaver/Woodcarver/Leatherworker as DEX (+8), and Glassblower/Potter as INT (+11). Use the actual action's ability, not INT for every tool. Lower check modifiers do not reduce unrelated spell effectiveness.

Fabricate still needs raw materials, its normal casting time and size limits, and appropriate proficiency for skilled craftsmanship. Intricate masonry is DM adjudication, not a blanket siege or damage bonus. Crafting access, special Utilize checks and spell effects are separate concepts.

Tinker's Magic temporary gear is distinct from magical replicas, elixirs, permanent potions and custom ammunition. No Crafter purchase discount or temporary-item grant remains after switching to Sage. Owning a tool does not itself grant proficiency or a free recipe.
