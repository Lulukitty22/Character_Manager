## Alchemist automatic grants and source confidence

Healing Word and Ray of Sickness are Artificer grants at Alchemist 3. The user first supplied the older Alchemist table. A later final-book-labelled [2025 transcription](https://dnd2024.wikidot.com/artificer:alchemist), located 2026-10-17, agrees with that pair. The grants are therefore no longer supported only by the older fallback, but the full official chapter remained gated. Secondary confirmation is not direct official full-book verification.

Use the recorded 2024 spell definitions rather than legacy pasted wording:

- Healing Word: Bonus Action, 60 ft, 2d4 + INT healing (11 average at INT 22); +2d4 per higher slot.
- Ray of Sickness: Action, 60 ft, spell attack +11; 2d8 Poison and Poisoned until the end of Carol's next turn on a hit, without an additional CON save; +1d8 per higher slot. Immunities apply. This Necromancy spell has no Evoker bonus, and Artificer 3 has no Artificer 5 Alchemical Savant.

These grants do not provide free casts or higher-level class features. Normal resources/components apply. Under the container rule they occupy ordinary entries with automatic-grant provenance; Workshop has room.
