## Accepted build summary

- Human (2024), Sage; Wizard-first Evoker Wizard 12 / Alchemist Artificer 3, total level 15. STR 8, DEX 16, CON 14, INT 22, WIS 10, CHA 10. INT includes the DM-granted Indomitable +2. HP 95, PB +5, initiative +8, speed 30 ft.
- War Caster, Skill Expert and Alert; Magic Initiate (Wizard) through Sage. Saves: STR −1, DEX +3, CON +2, INT +11, WIS +5, CHA +0. Concentration uses CON +2 with Advantage; DC 10 success is 87.75%.
- Base spell attack +11 and DC 19. Mage Armor AC 16, or 21 with Shield; Guise AC 18, or 23 with Shield. No Bladesong, Sorcerer or retired memory mechanics.
- Shared caster level 14; slots by level 1–7: 4/3/3/3/2/1/1. Actual spell access is Wizard through level 6 and Artificer through level 1. The seventh-level slot supports eligible upcasts. Arcane Recovery restores up to six combined slot levels, excluding slots of level 6+.
- Three permanent Enduring Spellbooks: Combat 16 entries, Cantrips 10, Workshop/Alchemy 6. Total 32 entries: 22 leveled spells and 10 cantrips. Each book has 16 spaces; eligible contents are ready. Mage Armor has the Magic Initiate free cast once per Long Rest. See [Books and Grants](../Class%20Reference/Carol%20Books%20and%20Grants.md).
- Arcana +16 and Investigation +16 with Expertise; History +11; Nature +11 is the selected Artificer proficiency; Insight, Medicine and Perception +5. Persuasion is untrained +0. Native tools: Calligrapher, Tinker, Alchemist and Herbalism. Manifold grants conditional artisan proficiency, not extra PB or global Advantage.
- Permanent Wand +1 and Manifold; known plans for Wand, Manifold, Goggles and Jug. Usual active replicas are Goggles of Night and Alchemy Jug. Aetherlock, Wand and Manifold use all three attunements. The granted rifle and two owned Bags of Holding are outside replica capacity.
- Equipment includes the kits, robes/books, component pouch, Identify's reusable 100 gp pearl, Guise's reusable 500 gp planar object, four Healing potions and writing/travel essentials. Other shopping candidates remain unselected. Do not invent historical gold debits or bag nesting.
- Replacement bodies remain lore/setpieces: one life, no automatic memory backup or revival; a replacement is roughly a level 8 reset. Reborn mechanics are retired.
